Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Xinfeng Rongchuang Technology Information Consulting Service Department ("we", "us") handles information in the apps we publish ("Apps").

Information We Collect

Our Apps are designed to work on your device. Unless an individual App's description states otherwise, we do not collect, store or sell your personal information, and we do not require you to create an account.

Data Stored on Your Device

Content you create in an App (such as photos, recordings or settings) is stored locally on your device or in your own iCloud account, and is not transmitted to our servers.

Permissions

Some features may request access to the camera, photo library, microphone or notifications. Access is used only to provide that feature and can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

If an App uses third-party services (for example, crash reporting or analytics), only the minimum anonymous data necessary is shared, and this will be disclosed in that App's privacy details on the App Store.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日

本隐私政策说明新丰县荣创科技信息咨询服务部（以下简称"我们"）发布的应用（以下简称"应用"）如何处理信息。

我们收集的信息

我们的应用以本地运行为主。除非某个应用的说明中另有声明，我们不收集、不存储、不出售你的个人信息，也不要求你注册账号。

设备上的数据

你在应用中创建的内容（如照片、录音、设置）保存在你的设备或你自己的 iCloud 中，不会上传到我们的服务器。

权限

部分功能可能请求相机、相册、麦克风或通知权限，仅用于实现对应功能，你可以随时在 iOS 设置中修改。

购买

应用内购买和订阅由 Apple 处理，我们不会接收或存储你的支付信息。

第三方服务

如果应用使用了第三方服务（例如崩溃统计），仅共享必要的匿名数据，并会在该应用的 App Store 隐私信息中说明。

儿童隐私

我们的应用不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。

政策更新
